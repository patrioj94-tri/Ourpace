The Road to Málaga

Forty weeks for Patri and Jean, from the first week of January to the finish line of Ironman 70.3 Málaga — with the Seville half marathon built in along the way, as a race to be run rather than an inconvenience to train through.

The shape of it

Each bar is one week. The dips every fourth week are deliberate — three weeks of loading, one of recovery, because the easy week is what makes the next three possible. Click any week to read it.

How it was put together

Seville is week 7. The first block is run-heavy with swimming and cycling kept ticking over, tapers into race day on 21 February, then takes a full easy week afterwards. Triathlon proper starts in March.

Targets are efforts, not paces. You two are not the same runner, and “threshold” means the right thing for both of you. Once Jean’s Strava is connected there is enough data to put real numbers on them.

Running off the bike starts in week 15 and never stops. It is the thing that decides a 70.3, and it cannot be learned in the last month. Week 28 is an Olympic-distance tune-up race, and week 36 is the full dress rehearsal: 90 km at race effort followed by 12 km, eating exactly what you plan to eat on the day.

Every session carries a distance or a duration. That is not decoration — it is what lets the app match a Strava upload to the session it answers, and tick it off without being asked.
